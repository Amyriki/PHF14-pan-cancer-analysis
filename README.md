# PHF14-pan-cancer-analysis
Pan-cancer analysis of PHF14-associated genomic and transcriptomic patterns using TCGA datasets.

## Research question

How do PHF14-associated genomic and transcriptomic patterns vary across
cancer types?

This project starts from PHF14. The aim is to identify genes associated with PHF14 expression, examine their genomic distribution, and investigate whether copy-number
alterations contribute to the observed expression patterns.

EGFR will be examined as a gene of particular interest based on previous
experimental observations, while keeping the genome-wide analysis
discovery-driven.

## Analysis plan

1. Characterize PHF14 expression across cancer types.
2. Identify genes correlated with PHF14 expression within each cancer type.
3. Compare PHF14-associated genes across cancers.
4. Examine the chromosomal distribution of PHF14-correlated genes.
5. Investigate PHF14-associated copy-number patterns.
6. Test how copy-number variation contributes to gene-expression associations.
7. Examine the PHF14–EGFR relationship across cancer types.
8. Explore biological pathways associated with PHF14.

## Initial cancer types

- LUAD — Lung adenocarcinoma
- LUSC — Lung squamous cell carcinoma
- STAD — Stomach adenocarcinoma
- PAAD — Pancreatic adenocarcinoma
- UCEC — Uterine corpus endometrial carcinoma

Additional TCGA cancer types may be included as the workflow develops.
